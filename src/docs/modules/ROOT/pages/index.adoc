= MyMediaScanner User Guide

:description: What MyMediaScanner does, who it is for, and where to go next in this guide.

MyMediaScanner is an app for cataloguing a personal collection of physical media.
Scan the barcode on a CD, DVD, Blu-ray, book, or video game and the app looks up its metadata, artwork, and details automatically, then adds it to your own collection database.
Everything is stored locally on your device, with an optional sync to a PostgreSQL server that you host yourself — there are no accounts and nothing is sent to a developer-run server.

MyMediaScanner is for anyone who wants a proper catalogue of what they own: knowing what is on the shelf, what has been lent out and to whom, what is still missing from a series, and how the collection has grown over time.

The app runs on five platforms — Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, and Linux — and adapts its interface to each.
Phones and tablets get camera scanning and a bottom navigation bar; desktop computers get a sidebar, webcam or USB barcode-scanner input, and extra screens suited to a bigger collection.
See xref:platform-differences.adoc[Platform Differences] for the full picture.
