= Wishlist
include::_attributes.adoc[]
:description: Track items you want to add to your collection, and let MyMediaScanner suggest what to add next.

The wishlist is a holding area for things you want but do not yet own.
An item on the wishlist behaves like any other entry in your collection — it has a cover, a title, and metadata — but it is flagged as not-yet-owned, so it is easy to tell apart from items you actually hold.

== Opening the wishlist

On mobile, open the *Library* screen and use the wishlist entry in its app bar.
On desktop, the wishlist has its own entry in the sidebar.

If you have not added anything yet, the screen shows a short prompt to scan or add an item and save it to the wishlist instead of the collection.

== Adding items to the wishlist

There are two ways to add something to the wishlist:

Scan or manual add::
When you scan a barcode or add an item by hand, the confirmation screen lets you choose whether to save it to your collection or straight to your wishlist, instead of always saving it as owned.
See xref:scanning.adoc[Scanning] and xref:manual-add.adoc[Manual Add].

Wishlist suggestions::
Every suggestion card has a button that adds that title straight to your wishlist without you having to scan or search for it.
See <<Wishlist Suggestions>> below.

== Marking an item as owned

Once you actually acquire something on your wishlist, open the wishlist screen and tap the *mark owned* action on that item's row.
It moves into your main collection immediately, and a confirmation message lets you know it has been added.

TIP: If you scan the barcode of an item that is already sitting on your wishlist, save it as owned as normal — there is no need to remove it from the wishlist first.

== Wishlist Suggestions

Beyond items you already know about, MyMediaScanner can suggest trending films and TV titles you do not yet own, ranked against your own taste — the genres you love, the tags you use, and the series you actively collect.

On desktop, *Wishlist suggestions* has its own entry in the sidebar.
On mobile, reach it from the *Suggestions for wishlist* link on the dashboard's *Recommended next* card.

Each suggestion shows a poster, title, year, and a short set of reason chips explaining why it was picked — for example, that it matches a genre you rate highly.
Tap the wishlist button on any card to add that title straight to your wishlist.

IMPORTANT: Wishlist suggestions are pulled from TMDB, so they require a TMDB API key.
See xref:api-keys.adoc[API Keys] to set one up.
If the list is empty even with a key configured, try rating a few items in your collection first — the suggestions are ranked using ratings and genres you have already recorded, so a brand-new collection has nothing yet to match against.
