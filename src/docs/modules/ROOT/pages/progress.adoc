= Progress
include::_attributes.adoc[]
:description: Track how far through a book, series, or film you are, and mark it finished when you are done.

Progress tracking lets you record where you are in a book, TV series, or film so you can pick up exactly where you left off.
It is entirely optional — an item you have not started stays out of the way and appears in your collection as normal.

== Starting progress on an item

Open the item's detail screen — see xref:item-details.adoc[Item Details] — and tap *Start reading* (books) or *Start watching* (everything else).

A short dialogue asks for:

Unit::
What you are counting — pages or chapters for a book, episodes or minutes for a TV series, minutes for everything else.
A sensible default is pre-selected based on the item's media type.

Total::
The total size in that unit, if you know it — for example the page count or episode count.
Leave it blank if you are not sure; you can still track your current position without a total.

== Updating your position

While an item is in progress, its detail screen shows *Update progress* instead of *Start*.
Tap it to enter your current position — if you set a total earlier, the value is capped at that total.

== Marking an item finished

Tap *Mark complete* on the item's detail screen once you have finished it.
The item drops off your in-progress list and is no longer suggested as something to pick up.

If you want to track a re-read or a re-watch, tap *Reset* to clear the item's progress back to not-started, then start it again.

NOTE: Only the most recent read-through is recorded — there is no history of multiple past readings or viewings.

== Seeing what is in progress

The dashboard's *Currently reading / watching* card lists a handful of your in-progress items with a progress bar for each, and tapping any of them opens that item's detail screen directly.

TIP: If nothing is in progress and you are not sure what to pick up next, the dashboard's *Surprise me* card offers a random pick from your collection.
